About mathΣτοά

mathΣτοά is a central place to find and share mathematical projects hosted in public repositories and to connect with their contributors. We welcome complete projects and work in progress.

Scope

mathΣτοά is particularly intended for research carried out with large language models (LLMs), but is not limited to such work. The focus is on a joint effort to understand and check work generated with an LLM. By making ongoing work visible, mathΣτοά aims to help researchers find one another and work together.

Such work may still be under development and may be based on natural language or formal proofs generated by an LLM. These repositories provide a place to develop a shared understanding of the work through explanations of parts of a proof, generalisations, and studies of the limitations of the results.

Submission criteria

The focus is on exposition of original research and formal proof verification.

To have your project listed, you should have:

  1. A public Git repository that contains an explanation of its aims and an introduction to the material, both written for human readers and kept up to date.
  2. Optionally, a DOI for an archived release of the repository that contains the version described in the listing.
    How to get a DOI for a GitHub repository with Zenodo.
  3. Substantive material that another person can engage with: notes, examples, partial results, explanations, or formal work.
  4. A clear description of progress and whether contributions are welcome.

Submissions receive a basic check for completeness before they are listed.

We encourage contributors to avoid duplicate listings and contact contributors to similar projects to discuss collaboration.

Moderation and responsibility

Listing does not certify mathematical correctness, establish originality, or constitute peer review.

Project maintainers are responsible for the content of their repositories. This includes claims, proofs, updates, and rules for contributions and reuse. Questions about that content should be directed to the project maintainers.
