About

mathΣτοά lists mathematical projects hosted in public Git repositories.

Scope

mathΣτοά is intended to index ongoing mathematical research projects that are not yet ready for publication in peer-reviewed journals or on preprint servers. It is particularly intended for research carried out with large language models (LLMs). Repositories listed on this website can include for example raw LLM-generated material to be improved with further exploration, or to be digested with human effort, or the formal proof verification of a mathematical result. We encourage the use of websites (such as GitHub Pages) to describe the aims of a project and give some information about the type of collaborative work that is being undertaken. Our goal is to reduce the pressure to publish results that have not yet been fully digested, or that may still be improved in future iterations. A public Git repository is accessible to anyone, but only authorised contributors can make changes.

Submission criteria

The focus is on exposition of original research and formal proof verification.

To have your project listed, you should have:

  1. A public Git repository containing one or more mathematical results in some form, preferably as a LaTeX source file with its associated PDF. This includes LLM-generated results, formal proof verifications, incomplete articles, and other mathematical work.
  2. An introduction to the repository’s aims, the form of collaborative or non-collaborative work it hosts, and the mathematical results already present, written for human readers and kept up to date. Preferably, this should be a website linked to the repository, although README files may also be accepted, subject to moderation.
  3. Optionally, a DOI for an archived release of the repository that contains the version described in the listing.
    How to get a DOI for a GitHub repository with Zenodo.

Submissions are reviewed before publication. Meeting these criteria does not guarantee a listing.

We encourage contributors to avoid duplicate listings and contact contributors to similar projects to discuss collaboration.

Moderation and responsibility

We may decline to list a project at our discretion, without giving a reason, except where an explanation is required by law or our complaints procedure.

Listing on this website does not certify mathematical correctness, establish originality, or constitute peer review.

Project maintainers are responsible for the content of their repositories. Questions about that content should be directed to the project maintainers.

Reports, complaints and feedback

To report a listing, suspected illegal content, a credit or copyright problem, a privacy or accessibility concern, or to appeal a moderation decision, email contact@math-stoa.org. No account is required. Include the listing URL and explain the concern. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information or copies of suspected illegal material.

Read how we handle reports and complaints.

Listings may be corrected or withdrawn for unlawful content, misleading attribution, lack of permission, or other breaches of the submission criteria. For corrections and withdrawals, we explain decisions where lawful and safe, and accept private appeals at the same address.

Suggestions and questions are also welcome at the email address above. For public feedback you can give feedback on GitHub. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account.
